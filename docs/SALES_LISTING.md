# Sala de Proyección — Sales Listing Draft

## Short pitch

**Sala de Proyección is a ready-to-customize AI video studio for the web.** It combines prompt-to-video production, project organization, multilingual narration, video assembly, credit accounting, billing integration and administrator controls in one product.

## What makes it valuable

- The product already has a working application structure rather than being only a UI mockup.
- Video generation is separated from the application layer so a buyer can change providers/models.
- Credits are reserved before generation and can be finalized or refunded after failure.
- PostgreSQL is used for account and billing records.
- Stripe Checkout/webhook integration points are already implemented.
- Render deployment configuration is included.
- The launch UI is deliberately focused on prompt-to-video for a clearer first product.
- The codebase is prepared for future image-to-video expansion without making it a launch dependency.

## Included in a sale

Unless the final agreement says otherwise:

- Complete source repository.
- Backend and web interface.
- Database/billing logic.
- Administrator functionality.
- Deployment configuration.
- Existing project documentation.
- Technical handoff instructions.

Third-party services, API accounts, domains and paid provider credits are **not** automatically included unless explicitly transferred in writing.

## Recommended asking price

**USD 7,500 asking price — negotiable.**

Target negotiation range: **USD 5,000–7,500** depending on buyer, scope of rights, support period and whether brand/domain assets are included.

A substantially higher valuation becomes easier to justify after the product has verified users, recurring revenue or a proven acquisition channel.

## Buyer profile

The best buyer is likely to be:

- an AI creator-tool company;
- a small SaaS operator;
- an agency wanting its own branded AI video platform;
- a developer/team looking for a video-generation product foundation;
- an entrepreneur who wants to replace the current provider with its own model infrastructure.

## Important disclosure

The product is an application layer around AI video providers; it does not itself guarantee a particular model's visual quality. Provider availability, quotas, pricing, licensing and commercial terms must be verified before production deployment.

The repository deliberately excludes secret API keys. The buyer should connect its own service accounts and production credentials.
